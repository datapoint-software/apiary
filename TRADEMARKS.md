# Trademark policy

> **Draft.** This policy has not yet been reviewed by a lawyer.

Apiary's [license](LICENSE) lets you use, change and share the software. It doesn't give you rights to the Datapoint name or logo (see term 3 in [NOTICE](NOTICE)). This policy explains what you can do with them, so that people can always tell what Datapoint made and what it didn't.

## What this policy covers

- The name **"Datapoint"**.
- The **Datapoint logo**, as provided in the `branding` directory.

## What you can do without asking

- **Keep the required attribution.** The admin interface must show "Based on Apiary by Datapoint - Sistemas de Informação, Lda." and the Datapoint logo (term 1 in [NOTICE](NOTICE)). Using the name and logo for that is always allowed.
- **Share unmodified copies** of Apiary with the name and logo as they come.
- **Describe your work truthfully**, for example "based on Apiary by Datapoint", "a fork of Apiary", or "works with Apiary".
- **Write about Apiary**, in articles, talks, tutorials or reviews.

## What you can't do without permission

- Use the Datapoint name or logo as the name or logo of **your own product, fork or service**.
- Suggest that Datapoint **made, endorses or supports** your product, fork or service when it doesn't.
- Use the Datapoint name or logo in a **domain name, company name or social media account**.
- **Change** the Datapoint logo, or combine it with other logos, except to resize it.

## Modified versions

If you share a modified version, mark it as modified, as term 2 in [NOTICE](NOTICE) requires, and give it a name of its own, so users don't mistake it for Datapoint's. For example, "Acme Proxy, based on Apiary" is fine. Calling it plain "Apiary", or "Datapoint Apiary", isn't.

The required attribution stays in the admin interface either way: credit for the original work, not a claim that Datapoint made your version.

## Asking for permission

For anything not covered here, contact Datapoint at https://datapoint.software/.
